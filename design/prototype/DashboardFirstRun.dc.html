How a box campaign works

1. Pick products Choose products, a category or a tag. Sold-out items hide themselves.
2. Set discount tiers 4 items 10% off, 6 items 15%, 12 items 25%. Shoppers see what the next item unlocks.
3. Place it on a page Add the Box Builder block or paste a shortcode. The box goes to the cart as one line.
