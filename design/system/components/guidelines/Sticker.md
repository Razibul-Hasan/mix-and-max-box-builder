# Sticker

The clickbait layer: a loud, uppercase pill in Bricolage for savings and urgency ("Save 25%", "Best value", "Hot deal").

- `tone`: `pop` (yellow, default), `max` (orange), `ink`. `size` sm on tiles and cards, md in heroes, lg for the one headline offer. `tilt` rotates -4°.
- Limit: one sticker per tile or card, one `lg` per page. Two stickers side by side cancel out.
- Text is a number or two words, never a sentence.
