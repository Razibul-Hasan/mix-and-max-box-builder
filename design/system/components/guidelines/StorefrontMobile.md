# StorefrontMobile

The shopper's phone builder for a Fixed box campaign: compact hero, steps, box-size pills, category chips, a 2-up grid and the sticky BoxSummaryBar. Working prototype.

**Features**
- Steps (Pick a size, Fill your box, Checkout) with only the current label shown.
- Box-size pills with stickers on Most popular and Best value; switching size trims the box to fit.
- Tiles stay full-featured at 2 columns; the remove control is always visible on touch.
- The bar shows how many slots remain and the discount the full box unlocks; the CTA reads "2 to go" until the box is full.
