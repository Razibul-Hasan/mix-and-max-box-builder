# Toggle

An on/off setting that applies immediately, with its label on the left and the switch on the right.

- Provide `label` (what is on: "Countdown timer") and `description` (what the shopper will see).
- Controlled (`checked` + `onChange(next)`) or uncontrolled (`defaultChecked`).
- Use Checkbox, not Toggle, inside forms that need a Save, and for the storefront gift option.
