# Badge

One or two words of status or type, in a pill.

- `tone`: `success` Active, `info` Scheduled, `neutral` Draft/Ended, `warning` Ending soon, `critical` Paused or error, `brand` campaign type, `deal` a product promo, `dark` plan tier.
- Add `dot` for campaign status so status reads in tables at a glance. The word is required: colour alone never carries status.
- Use Sticker, not Badge, for the loud savings callouts.
