# TierProgress

The money line: a sentence that says what the next item unlocks, a Max Orange track with tier markers, and a tier legend.

- Provide `count`, `tiers` (`{qty, label}`), `size` (track length). Messages: "Add 2 more to get 15% off" → "10% off unlocked. Add 1 more…" → "You unlocked 25% off. That's the max deal."
- `compact` drops the legend (bars, tight panels); `inverse` for the dark BoxSummaryBar.
